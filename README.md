# Zahra Razavi Portfolio Website

This repository contains the source code for my personal portfolio website:

[zahra-razavi.github.io](https://zahra-razavi.github.io/)

The website showcases my work as a Product Designer, UX/UI Designer, and startup founder. It includes selected case studies, product design work, research-driven projects, and experiments related to digital products, AI tools, healthcare, FinTech, logistics, and service design.

## About the Website

This portfolio is designed to present my professional background, design process, and selected projects in a clear and structured way. The goal is to make my work easy to explore for recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and potential partners.

The website includes:

- Product design and UX/UI case studies
- Startup and founder-led product work
- Research-based design projects
- Visual design and branding examples
- Contact and professional profile links

## Website Goals

The main goals of this website are to:

- Present my product design experience professionally
- Highlight my design thinking, process, and business impact
- Show selected projects in a clear and accessible format
- Keep the website lightweight, fast, and easy to maintain
- Support future updates as my portfolio grows
