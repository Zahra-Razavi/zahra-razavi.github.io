About

I’m Zara, a product designer shaped by design, research, and startup life.

I fell in love with design when I was 15. It quickly became more than a field I wanted to study. It became a way to understand people, systems, and how ideas turn into real experiences.

I started in mathematics and physics, with engineering as the obvious path ahead. But I chose design instead.

Since then, I’ve spent four years studying design, three years researching HCI and UX during my master’s, and seven years designing brands, products, and user experiences for startups.

That path brought me here: designing UX and AI products that make complex experiences clearer, simpler, and more human.
